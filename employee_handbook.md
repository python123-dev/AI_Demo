# Employee Handbook

## Work Hours

The standard work week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours. Core work hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which all employees are expected to be available. Employees may schedule the remaining hours flexibly between 7:00 AM and 7:00 PM.

## Employee Benefits

Full-time employees receive health, dental, and vision insurance starting on their first day. The company offers 20 days of paid vacation per year, 10 paid sick days, and a 401(k) plan with a 4% employer match. An annual learning budget of $1,000 is available for courses and conferences.

## Disciplinary Measures

Policy violations are handled in steps: a verbal warning, a written warning, a final warning, and then termination. Serious misconduct such as harassment, theft, or falsifying records may lead to immediate termination after review by HR.

## General Queries

Questions about this handbook can be sent to hr@example.com or raised with your manager. The handbook is reviewed and updated every year, and employees are notified of any changes by email.
