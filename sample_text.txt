The author developed an interest in computers at an early age. As a teenager, the author spent most afternoons reading magazines about electronics and dreaming of building machines. Around 1980, the author purchased an Apple II computer and used it to learn programming and explore computing.

The author's first programs were simple games written in BASIC. Over time, the author moved on to writing small tools for managing data and drawing graphics on the screen.

Apart from computers, the author also enjoyed painting. Weekends were spent in a small studio, working on landscapes and still life studies. The author believed that making software and making paintings were similar, since both required patience and a good eye for detail.

Later, the author wrote essays about startups, programming languages, and the nature of work. These essays were widely shared among engineers and founders.
